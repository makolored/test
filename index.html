Who we are

Marstek Energy PH is the local supplier of Marstek home battery and solar storage systems. We bring in the units, set them up, and stay reachable after the sale — which matters more than the spec sheet when a system is going into your house.

Brownouts are routine here and Meralco rates are among the highest in the region. A home battery answers both: it carries your household through an outage, and it lets you store power when it's cheap instead of drawing it when it's expensive.

We work through agents across Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao, so there's someone in your area who can walk you through sizing, delivery and installation in person.
